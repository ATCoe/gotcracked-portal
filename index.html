Add a staff member

Creates their Supabase account, assigns a GotCracked login, and generates a one-use Discord invite.

Simple onboarding

  1. Give the employee the generated GotCracked login and temporary password.
  2. Send the one-use Discord invite included in the package.
  3. The employee joins Discord, signs in, and replaces the temporary password.
  4. Supabase records each onboarding checkpoint for the whole team.

Discord usernames identify the intended employee. Discord itself does not allow a bot to DM a username that has not joined the server.
